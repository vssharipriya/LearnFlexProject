# Demo walkthrough

1. Sign in using one of the demo accounts listed in `docs/DEMO-ACCOUNTS.md`.
2. Open Learn and select the student's class, subject and chapter.
3. Review the chapter lesson and example.
4. Complete the adaptive question set; the system routes foundation, reinforcement, standard and challenge questions.
5. Review the feedback and follow-up check.
6. Teachers can view their assigned class dashboard.

The active curriculum is CBSE-only. The catalogue uses NCERT chapter titles for the listed editions and separately labels curricular activity modules. Schools must confirm subject and language choices. Questions are original practice drafts, not official CBSE exam items.


## Round 2 judging demonstration

7. During a question, reveal Hint 1, Hint 2 and Hint 3 in order. Explain that hints are optional and score-neutral.
8. Submit a wrong answer in a mapped question to show the misconception hypothesis, teaching action and separate verification question.
9. Switch the browser offline after loading the app and open a previously saved topic pack. Reconnect and confirm the local-first session remains available and authenticated sync retries.
10. As a teacher, open Learning insights to show class-scoped gaps, follow-up outcomes and the evidence-quality summary. Use synthetic accounts only for this demo; paired pilot evidence requires real, school-approved consent and real attempts.
11. Switch between English, Telugu and Hindi. Point out that translation coverage varies and drafts require native subject-teacher review.
12. Do not present synthetic demo history as learning-impact evidence. Do not claim AI capability; generative AI is intentionally not part of this release.
